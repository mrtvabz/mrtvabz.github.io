How the route works

Select a stop to see its role in the shipment.

Incheon is the departure point. The vehicle is prepared for sea transport and loaded onto a RoRo vessel.

Shipping stages

From vessel to railway

01

RoRo to Yantai

The vehicle leaves Incheon on a RoRo vessel and arrives at the port of Yantai.

02

Preparation and documents

The required transit documents are prepared for passage through China and Kazakhstan.

03

Rail to Altynkol

The vehicle then travels by rail to Altynkol, where it is handed over to the recipient.

Questions and answers

What to know about this route

What does the $2,500 price include?

Vehicle loading, transit documentation for China and Kazakhstan, RoRo transport from Incheon to Yantai, and rail transport to Altynkol.

How long does delivery take?

The estimated transit time is 15–17 days. Actual timing depends on vessel and train schedules, cargo handling, and required procedures.

What documents are required?

Requirements depend on the vehicle and shipment terms. A manager will confirm the current document list before dispatch.

Where is the vehicle handed over?

Altynkol is the arrival and vehicle handover point. Further delivery to your final city must be arranged separately.
