FAQ

Destination, cargo type, preferred timeline, and contacts. If it’s a car, VIN helps too.

Can you provide reinforced packing?

Yes. We offer standard and reinforced securing/packing options — depending on your task and budget.

How will I know the status?

We provide updates at key stages: preparation → documents → port → dispatch → arrival.
