FAQ

Quick answers to common questions.

What do you need from me to get started?

Destination, cargo type, preferred timing and contact details. A VIN also helps if you are shipping a car.

Can you provide extra packing?

Yes. We discuss securing and packing options based on the cargo and transport conditions.

How will I know the status?

Our team provides updates at key stages: preparation, documents, port, departure and arrival.

Cargo preparation

What to discuss before container shipping

Container shipping lets us place a car or other cargo inside a container. Before departure, we confirm dimensions, condition, packing requirements and how it will be secured. These details affect preparation, paperwork and route choice.

If you plan to ship spare parts or other cargo with the car, let us know early. We will check how to document and place them and agree on the work before loading.
