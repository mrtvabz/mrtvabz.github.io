How to use these English pages

1) Keep your current Russian pages in the root of your site (same as now).
2) Create a folder named: en
3) Upload the files from this package into /en on your hosting.
   - index-en.html should be renamed to index.html inside /en (same for others),
     OR keep the -en names and update your menu links later.
4) IMPORTANT: your HTML currently links to assets like style.css, images, and JS using relative paths.
   If you place the English pages inside /en, you have two safe options:
   A) Copy the same assets (style.css, pogruzka.css, JS files, images) into /en as well (no code change).
   B) Or change asset paths to ../style.css etc (this edits code).
