News

News: South Korean used-car exporters are facing serious logistics challenges due to instability around the Strait of Hormuz and Red Sea shipping routes. Freight costs for vehicle exports have surged up to four times higher than normal levels, while some shipments heading toward Dubai and North Africa have been delayed or rerouted through alternative ports such as Sri Lanka. The situation has heavily affected exporters operating through Incheon Port, one of Asia’s largest used-car export hubs.

ABOUT THE COMPANY

MaxWin Logistics

We’re a team in Korea that helps clients buy and ship cars and cargo quickly, transparently, and safely. We follow a clear process: inspection → documents → photo report → shipment (RoRo / container).

MaxWin Logistics is a logistics company founded in 2020, operating in Korea and specializing in exporting cars and cargo by sea. We help clients buy and ship cars and spare parts from Korea quickly, transparently, and safely, building the process so it’s clear even for those doing international shipping for the first time. Our work follows a simple and logical scheme: inspection → preparation → photo report → shipment (RoRo or container). Every stage is controlled so the client always understands where their cargo is. We handle communication with shipping lines and ports, show the process through photos and videos, and support the shipment until it arrives. Our goal is to give the client a sense of control and confidence at every step. We explain everything in simple language — without pressure and without “smart words”. PREMIUM

Cargo safety

We pay special attention to keeping the vehicle safe at every stage of transportation. During loading and securing, we use special lashing straps and fixation to reliably hold the vehicle and prevent shifting during sea transport. If needed, we provide reinforced packing and additional fixation, choosing the solution depending on the vehicle type and shipping format (RoRo or container). The loading and securing process is accompanied by photo control, so you can be sure the vehicle is prepared and secured correctly. Our goal is simple — the cargo arrives safely, with no surprises.

FAQ

Useful

Where does MaxWin Logistics ship from in South Korea?

We primarily operate out of the ports of Incheon and Busan. Our headquarters is located in Incheon, allowing us to manage the export of vehicles and cargo directly from Korea’s most active shipping hubs.

Useful

Which countries do you export cars to?

We specialize in the CIS region and post-Soviet countries. This includes regular routes to Russia (Vladivostok), Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, and Kyrgyzstan. We handle all necessary export documentation for these specific customs zones.

Useful

How long is the shipping time from Korea to Vladivostok?

The sea transit time is approximately 2–3 days. However, the total time depends on the specific vessel schedule and the time required for customs clearance in the port of departure.

Useful

What shipping methods do you offer for vehicle exports?

We offer two main methods: RoRo (Roll-on/Roll-off) for drivable vehicles and Container shipping for high-value cars, non-runners, or shipments that include spare parts.
