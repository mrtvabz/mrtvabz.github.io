About us

Logistics with a clear view of every step

MaxWin Logistics is a team in South Korea working with exports of cars, spare parts and cargo since 2020. We guide customers through the process, from choosing a shipping method and preparing the car to handling documents and departure.

With international shipping, you need to know more than the route: you need to know what's already been done to your car. We stay in touch, explain the next stage and provide photos and videos of preparation and loading. Customers see the real work, not just a status update.

We work with ports and shipping lines in Korea. For each inquiry, we confirm the car, destination and transport terms, then suggest a suitable plan.

How we work

More detail. Fewer unknowns.

Every shipment is different. Here's what we discuss and check before a car leaves Korea.

01 / SHIPPING METHOD

RoRo or container

RoRo carries a car on a specialized vessel. A container is used when cargo needs enclosed placement, additional securing or particular preparation. We compare options for the car, route and shipping terms rather than suggest one format for everyone.

Learn more about RoRoLearn more about containers
02 / DOCUMENTS

Paperwork without confusion

Export requires agreed details about the car, sender and recipient. We explain what information is needed, check documents before port handover and tell you when they're ready. Requirements depend on the route and cargo, so we confirm them for each shipment.

Ask about documents
03 / VISIBILITY

Photos and videos, not guesswork

Car handover, preparation and loading are stages you can see. Photos and videos help confirm the car's condition and placement before departure. We agree on the report beforehand; it depends on the shipping method and access to each stage.

See loading videos
04 / CARGO CARE

Preparation and securing

The car or cargo must be placed correctly before departure. Depending on the method, we agree on securing, packing and any extra support needed. We discuss this before loading so you know what's included and what may require separate work.

Destinations

From Korea, by an agreed route

MaxWin Logistics arranges shipments from South Korea to a range of destinations. For sea freight via Vladivostok, we discuss the onward land leg separately. For Kazakhstan, the Incheon → Yantai → Altynkol route uses RoRo to Yantai and then rail to the car handover point.

The route, timing and services depend on the destination. Before booking, we confirm the final point, required documents and where the car will be handed to the recipient.

Questions and answers

What to know before shipping

Which shipping methods do you offer?

We use RoRo and containers. The right method depends on the car, cargo and route.

Can I ship spare parts or other cargo?

Yes, we can ship spare parts and other cargo from Korea. We confirm the method, packing and documents for each inquiry.

Will I receive photos and videos?

We show car handover, preparation and loading. The exact report is confirmed for each shipment.

Which documents will I need?

Documents depend on the route and car. Your manager will provide the current list before processing.

Will you deliver the car to my city?

The handover point depends on the route and agreed terms. On the route via Yantai, for example, the car is handed over in Altynkol. Onward delivery is discussed separately.

How can I find the next departure date?

Contact the MaxWin Logistics team. We will check available sailings and confirm current terms.
