Information needed for shipping

  1. Recipient's full name.
  2. Recipient's address, including city and postal code if required.
  3. Document details required for the specific route.
  4. If a document copy is needed, your manager will explain how to send it.

Please note

  1. Whether a car can be loaded depends on its condition and specifications.
  2. Personal belongings inside the car must be agreed separately; documents or a declaration may be required.
  3. Photos before loading are included in the agreed report.
  4. Ask in advance about additional photos after handover at the port.

Before shipping

When RoRo is the right fit

With RoRo, the car drives onto a specialized vessel. This works for a roadworthy car when its specifications and the route allow it to be driven aboard. For shipments via Vladivostok, we agree on the sea leg, documents and next steps separately.

Before booking, we need to confirm the car's condition, destination and information needed for the paperwork. Our team explains port handover, space availability and which materials you can receive after loading.
