RoRo Service
RoRo

Max Win Logistics arranges RoRo car shipping from Korea via the port of Busan to Vladivostok. After arrival in Vladivostok, we support onward delivery to Central Asian countries such as Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, and others.

Photo report Document support Tracking / communication

Steps

  1. Recipient’s full name
  2. Residential address (city, street, building, apartment).
  3. Passport details (passport series/number).
  4. If needed — a photo of the passport (photo page and registration page). This information is required to prepare the documents. Thank you for your cooperation.

Reminder

  1. Suitable for all vehicle types
  2. Personal items are allowed if the required documents/declaration are provided (if needed).
  3. Photos before loading — included
  4. Photos after gate-in — optional

FAQ

Quick answers to the questions we get most often.

Do you provide delivery to Uzbekistan / Kazakhstan after Vladivostok?

Yes. After arrival in Vladivostok, we can support transit and inland delivery arrangements to Central Asia (on request).

Do you provide a photo report?

Yes. We provide a photo report at key stages of the process.

Do you help with export documents?

Yes. We support export documentation and guide the process from Korea to departure.
